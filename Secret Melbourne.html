This scenic trail along the Bass Coast has opened three new lookouts, just in time for summer

Head out of Melbourne and take advantage of the long, sunny days by visiting one of these lookouts on the Yallock-Bulluk Coastal Trail.

We love breathing in the salty sea air and watching the waves on one of the many coastal walks around Victoria. And something that we’re really excited about is the Yallock-Bulluk Coastal Trail. Stretching across 32 kilometres, this upcoming trail will weave its way from San Remo to Cape Paterson, using a mix of new and existing tracks. It’s located in the Yallock-Bulluk Marine and Coastal Park, which covers the coastline between San Remo and Inverloch. Construction is well underway, and things are shaping up nicely, with three brand-new lookouts for us to check out.

New lookouts on Yallock-Bulluk coastal trail

Named for the Yallock-Bulluk Clan of the Bunurong People, the park’s trail is being built in stages, with lookouts, parking and picnic facilities.

Stage one links San Remo and Cape Paterson via an upgraded George Bass Coastal Walk; reaching Inverloch (45km) depends on funding.

Lookouts at Punchbowl, Eagles Nest and The Arch are already open.

Built with the Bunurong Land Council Aboriginal Corporation, they offer artwork, signage and Elders’ audio stories via QR code.

Further sections will follow; project updates are online.
