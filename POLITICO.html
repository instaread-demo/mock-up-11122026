White House threatens criminal investigation into Democrats’ oversight efforts

A letter from the Trump administration’s top lawyer accuses Democrats of potentially crossing constitutional guardrails.

By Kyle Cheney and Chris Marquette 11/11/2026, 9:08AM ET Updated: 11/11/2026, 9:53AM ET

The White House’s top lawyer is threatening a criminal investigation of Democrats for conducting what he alleges are illegitimate investigations into the executive branch — a stark escalation from the Trump administration as congressional Democrats prepare to launch an oversight onslaught when they assume the majority in January.

White House counsel Will Scharf, in a letter to incoming House Oversight Committee Chair Robert Garcia, says Democrats’ repeated public promises to use their new majority to investigate the president and his allies could cross constitutional limits — and even amount to illegal threats.

The letter, dated Monday and released Wednesday, says the White House is watching what it calls Democratic threats to misuse the legislative process.

Scharf cites remarks by Garcia and other Democrats and warns they could stray into “blatant criminality,” inviting an executive-branch inquiry into Congress.

The warning mirrors posts in which Trump has suggested Democrats could face criminal consequences; the Justice Department typically handles oversight matters with Congress.

Subpoenas must serve a legislative purpose, but courts have long shown deference to congressional investigators.

Garcia said in a statement that Congress’ authority stems from Article I and that it will not be deterred by “baseless threats.”
