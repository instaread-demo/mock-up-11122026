Local representatives will likely lose committee titles with House takeover

By HANK ROWLAND The Brunswick News Nov 11, 2026 0

A takeover of the state House by Democrats will lead to a loss of committee status to the three Republicans who represent Brunswick and the Golden Isles.

State Reps. Buddy DeLoach of Townsend, Steven Sainz of St. Marys and Rick Townsend of St. Simons Island won their re-election bids for another term that will begin in January, but they will more than likely lose their committee titles of leadership. They may even find themselves on different committees.
