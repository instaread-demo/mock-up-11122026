Australians planning their next getaway can now snag coach fares for as little as $10, with FlixBus celebrating its first birthday by slashing prices across its East Coast network.

The coach operator is marking one year in Australia with a seat sale running until 23rd November 2026, covering travel between 10th November 2026 and 30th April 2027.

There’s no discount code required. Travellers can book directly through the FlixBus website or app, although $10 seats are limited.

From two routes to 40+ destinations

FlixBus launched in November 2025 with two Sydney links. It now runs six major hubs across NSW, Victoria, the ACT and Queensland, with routes up to Cairns, a Parramatta to Canberra service and seasonal Snowy Mountains trips.

Where can you go for $10?

Routes on offer include:

  • Sydney to Brisbane: Port Macquarie, Byron Bay and Surfers Paradise.
  • Sydney to Canberra: the capital’s museums and gardens.
  • Sydney to Melbourne: via Canberra and Albury.
  • Brisbane to Bundaberg: Noosa Heads, Rainbow Beach and K’gari.
  • Bundaberg to Cairns: Airlie Beach, Townsville and the reef.
  • Parramatta to Canberra: via the new airport and Goulburn.

FlixBus plans to expand further in Australia

Managing Director Yvan Lefranc-Morin describes the sale as a thank-you to first-year passengers and says FlixBus aims to grow by partnering with more local bus operators.

How to book $10 FlixBus tickets

Book by 23rd November 2026 for trips up to 30th April 2027 via the FlixBus website or app; no code needed.
