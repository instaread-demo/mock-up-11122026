Books Like The Imperfect Psychic

Although psychic abilities are commonly dismissed as pseudoscience with no proof of existence, there has been no shortage of people claiming to possess these gifts. From communication with spirits to predicting the future, the powers attributed to psychics date back to ancient times. Of course, these extraordinary powers also make for exciting stories, which is why psychics often play a central role in cozy mystery novels. Ashley King’s The Imperfect Psychic is a good example: Charlotte Vale finally opens her dream bed and breakfast, then gets pulled into a murder she has to solve with gifts that rarely work as planned.

Murder by the Brush

By S. E. Babin

The first Psychic Cleaner Cozy Mystery sends a broke, newly single Kate to haunted Hallow Hills, Tennessee, where her psychic cleaning business and one stubborn ghost lead her into a murder case.

Abby Cooper: Psychic Eye

By Victoria Laurie

In this series opener, intuitive Abby misses both a client’s death and the fact that her new date leads the investigation, leaving her facing a loose killer and police who doubt her.

Beach Blanket Homicide

By Maggie March

Whispering Bay muffin baker and psychic Lucy McGuffin finds a body, sees her brother become the suspect, and works to clear him despite new deputy Travis Fontaine’s objections.

A Deadly Delivery

By April Fernsby

Mid-divorce and reconciling with her sister Erin, lifelong psychic Karis Booth has a vision of a woman named Carmel being killed and investigates, only to find her ex-boyfriend running the case.

Death At First Sight

By Lena Gregory

Former psychiatrist Cass Donovan runs a psychic shop on Bay Island until Marge Callahan, who mocked her reading for Marge’s daughter Ellie, turns up dead and Cass becomes a suspect.

A Vision a Day Keeps the Killer Away

By Kelly Hashway

Psychic Piper Ashwell helps her detective father search for kidnapped heiress Veronica Castell, knowing the abductor plans to kill her once the ransom is paid.

Finding Peace of Mind

By Miranda Rose Barker

After a car accident, art student Tansy Silver can suddenly hear her Aunt Vivian’s French bulldog, Hank, and puts that gift to work when a young boy goes missing in Pine Ridge.

Petal to the Metal

By Annabel Chase

Mia Thorne, 42, retreats to her late aunt Hazel’s Pennsylvania cottage, finds a body in the garden, and must revive her own dormant psychic skills to clear her name.

Mediums & Murder

By Elle Wren Burke

In the first Prickly Pear Psychic Mystery, Alara foresees a friend’s murder in her Arizona town and solves it with help from her long-lost Aunt Irene, who shares the family gift.
