St. Mary in Crown Point hosts its biggest Spaghetti Supper since COVID for its 65th year

Each year, St. Mary Catholic Community School brings together current students, parents, teachers, alumni and the Crown Point community for its annual Spaghetti Supper that features more than pasta.

This year's supper was the 65th, and it was the biggest one the school has hosted since the COVID-19 pandemic.

The fundraiser once more drew the school and the wider city together around a shared meal, carrying on a long-running St. Mary tradition.

Volunteers handled the cooking behind the scenes, while others plated food for guests who chose to eat in.

Diners could stay for the meal or pick it up, and students helped carry orders out to people waiting in their cars.

Students of all ages took part, from students helping diners to first-graders working through their plates of pasta.
