GOP crossroads

We were struck by how conciliatory the Georgia GOP’s postelection report was. So were plenty of frustrated Republican activists.

The 39-page Georgia GOP briefing documents the party’s election wipeout in painstaking detail and suggests Republicans can find common ground with Gov.-elect Keisha Lance Bottoms on grocery tax relief, pre-K funding and rural hospital incentives.

Beyond that, whole sections of the document walk through the incoming Democratic agenda, under headings that tie House Democrats and Bottoms to affordability and cost-of-living relief.
