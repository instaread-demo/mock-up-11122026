Although The Beatles enjoyed massive success that endures to this day, it can be argued Paul McCartney is the most successful of the fab four. His career has continued both solo and with his rock band “Wings”. He has released 27 albums since The Beatles went their separate ways.

Beyond songwriting, he played bass, guitars, keyboards and drums in The Beatles; the Mellotron, his oddest choice, opens Strawberry Fields Forever.

JPaul McCartney demonstrates the MellotronJezNashMusicProduction Watch on YouTube

In 2005, at the Chaos and Creation at Abbey Road session in London, he called it a 60s synthesizer forerunner whose keys trigger taped strings, trumpet or flutes, “like a tape cassette.”

He concluded it was designed for cabarets, letting one instrument stand in for a band.
